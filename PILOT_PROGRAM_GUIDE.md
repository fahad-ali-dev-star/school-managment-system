# 🏫 Pilot Program Playbook: Turnkey Guide to Onboard 2–3 Local Schools

This guide provides everything you need to launch a 30-day pilot program with 2–3 local partner schools to gather live case studies, testimonials, and convert them into paying annual SaaS subscriptions.

---

## 🎯 Pilot Program Goals & Strategy
1. **Target Audience:** Private primary, middle, and secondary schools (150 – 1,000 students) currently using paper registers or disorganized Excel sheets.
2. **Offer:** **"30-Day Zero-Risk Free Pilot with White-Glove Onboarding & Free Data Migration"**.
3. **Outcome:** A locked-in customer, video/written testimonial, and case-study metrics for your landing page.

---

## 📅 The 4-Week Pilot Roadmap

| Week | Focus Area | What You Do | School Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1** | **Setup & Migration** | • Onboard the school via Super-Admin (`/super-admin`)<br>• Use the new **Bulk CSV Importer** to upload their student list<br>• Set up classes, sections & teacher logins | Zero data entry headaches for the principal |
| **Week 2** | **Daily Attendance & SMS** | • Train teachers on PWA attendance marking (mobile & desktop)<br>• Enable automated WhatsApp / SMS absence alerts | Parents receive instant notifications; immediate impression |
| **Week 3** | **Fees & Finance** | • Generate current month pending fee slips<br>• Send WhatsApp fee reminders to parents<br>• Record cash & online payments | Principal sees instant reduction in pending fee dues |
| **Week 4** | **Exams & Conversion** | • Input exam marks & generate 1-click PDF Report Cards<br>• Present the 30-day ROI analytics dashboard to the Principal<br>• Transition to Paid Annual / Monthly Subscription | Principal signs annual agreement; records testimonial |

## 📝 Structured Feedback Survey for Pilot Schools

At day 25 of the pilot, share this brief 5-minute survey with the Principal and staff:

### For the Principal / Administrator:
1. *How much administrative time did your staff save each week using the ERP?* (e.g. 5–10 hrs, 10–20 hrs, 20+ hrs)
2. *Did you notice an improvement in parent fee payments and response time?* (Scale 1–10)
3. *What was your favorite feature?* (e.g., WhatsApp alerts, Bulk CSV import, AI assistant, PDF report cards)
4. *Would you recommend this system to other school leaders?* (Quote for landing page)

### For Teachers:
1. *How easy was marking daily attendance on desktop and mobile?* (Scale 1–5)
2. *Did the offline mode help when the internet was down?* (Yes / No / Comments)
3. *How much faster was exam marks entry compared to your previous manual process?*

## 🚀 Quick Super-Admin Onboarding Checklist

When onboarding a new school in the Super-Admin Center (`/super-admin`):
1. Navigate to `/super-admin?key=YOUR_SUPER_ADMIN_KEY`.
2. Click **"+ Onboard New School"**.
3. Fill in:
   - School Name (e.g. *Horizon Model Academy*)
   - Principal Name, Email, and Phone
   - Assign Plan: `Pro` (for pilot period)
4. Go to **Students** tab (`/students`) and click **"📥 Import CSV"** to upload the school's roster in seconds.
5. Provide the principal with their login credentials and link to their white-labeled portal!
